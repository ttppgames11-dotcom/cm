Privacy Policy

Connect Maratha (the "app") · Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Connect Maratha mobile app and its server collect, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. Contact: support@connectmaratha.com.

Data we collect

DataWhy
Name, phone number, email addressTo create and sign in to your account. If you give an email address, we send one welcome email when your account is created. Email is optional if you give a phone number.
PasswordTo sign in. Only a one-way hash is stored; we never store your password in readable form.
Password-reset codeIf you use "Forgot password", we email a one-time code (OTP) to your registered address. Only a one-way hash of the code is kept, for at most 10 minutes.
Profile details you choose to enter: city, district, profession, business, education, skills, interests, "about"To show your community profile to other members.
Profile photo (only if you add one)To show your picture to other members next to your name, posts and comments. You can change or remove it at any time in the app.
Photos you attach to posts (only if you add one)To show the photo in the community feed as part of your post.
Membership ID, tier and roleTo run the membership.
Google account name and email (only if you use "Sign in with Google")To sign you in. We verify the Google sign-in token and do not store it.
Posts, comments, likes, reports and blocks you makeTo provide the community feed and keep it safe.
IP address (in server logs)Security and preventing abuse, such as repeated login attempts.
Crash and error reports: what went wrong, the app version, your phone model and Android version, and a random installation IDTo find and fix crashes. Reports do not contain your name, phone number, email address, password or anything you write in the app.

Your phone number and email are not shown to other members.

Posts are meant to be read: a member can share a post as a link, and anyone who opens that link can read the post text, see its photo if it has one, and see the name of the member who wrote it. Phone numbers and email addresses are never shown there. When a post is deleted, its link stops working and its photo is deleted.

We do not collect your location, contacts, microphone data or advertising identifiers. The app has no ads and does not track you or build advertising profiles. The app does not read your photo gallery: only a photo that you yourself choose (or take with the camera) for your profile or for a post is uploaded.

How data is stored and protected

  • All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Your sign-in session is kept in your phone's secure storage (Android Keystore).
  • Data is stored on servers we rent from our hosting provider (Hostinger) and is accessible only to authorised administrators.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. Data is processed only by:

  • our hosting provider (Hostinger), which runs our server, database and email;
  • Cloudflare, which carries encrypted traffic between the app and our server and provides DNS;
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), which receives the app's crash and error reports so that we can fix problems;
  • Google, only if you choose "Sign in with Google".

We may disclose data if required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Security & Privacy → Delete account) or by following the steps on our account deletion page.

When you delete your account, your profile, sessions, posts, comments, likes, group memberships and listings are deleted immediately. Records that other members depend on (for example a meeting with another member) are kept with your name removed. Deleted data remains in backups for up to 30 days, after which it is gone.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

You can view and edit your profile in the app, delete your account, or email us to request a copy of your data or to raise a concern. We reply within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
