1. Explicit Prohibition of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Connect Maratha strictly prohibits all forms of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) across the platform.

Prohibited content and conduct includes, but is not limited to:

  • Any visual, audio, or textual depiction or facilitation of sexually explicit conduct involving a minor.
  • Child Grooming & Solicitation: Any attempt to contact, communicate with, or manipulate a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Sextortion: Coercing or threatening any individual, especially minors, using sexual or intimate media.
  • Commercial Sexual Exploitation: Advertising, organizing, or promoting the sexual exploitation or trafficking of children.
  • AI & Synthetic Material: Computer-generated, deepfake, or simulated sexual depictions of minors.

2. Content Moderation, Immediate Takedowns & Enforcement

Connect Maratha utilizes automated detection, hash matching, and human review to identify and eradicate harmful content.

  • Immediate Deletion: Any content identified as CSAE or CSAM is deleted immediately and permanently.
  • Account Ban: Users attempting to upload, share, or request such content face immediate and permanent bans across all associated devices and credentials.
  • Evidence Preservation: Logs and cryptographic evidence are securely preserved for law enforcement proceedings.

3. Law Enforcement Cooperation (NCMEC & POCSO)

In accordance with domestic and international legal standards (including the POCSO Act, IT Act 2000, and global safety standards), Connect Maratha reports all suspected instances of CSAM directly to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to statutory Indian law enforcement authorities.
