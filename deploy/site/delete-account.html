Delete your Connect Maratha account

In the app

  1. Open Connect Maratha and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Security & Privacy → Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your password (or Google sign-in) and type DELETE.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email support@connectmaratha.com from the email address on your account (or include your registered phone number) with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify that the request is yours and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Deleted: your profile, sign-in sessions, posts, comments, likes, group memberships, event RSVPs, notifications, blocks, business listings and job posts.
  • Kept with your name removed: records shared with other members (such as meetings and referrals), quiz leaderboard entries, and reports you made about content.
  • Backups: deleted data stays in encrypted backups for up to 30 days, then it is gone.

See the privacy policy for details.
